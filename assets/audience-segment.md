# Audience Segment — Daily Nurture Subscribers

## Segment Name

Daily Nurture — Expert Entrepreneurs (OneTake AI subscribers)

## Opt-In Origin

Subscribers opted in through one or more of the following:
- OneTake AI free trial or freemium plan sign-up
- Lead magnet (video content guide, free training, checklist)
- Webinar registration for one of Sébastien Night's live training events
- Content upgrade from YouTube, podcast, or blog content
- Referral from existing customers

They chose to receive Sébastien's emails. They are not a purchased or rented list.

## Topic Contract

This list signed up to receive practical content about:
- Creating professional video content without technical skills or a production team
- Growing their online presence and reaching more customers through video
- Automating content creation to save time and gain freedom
- Turning their expertise into scalable online training products and courses

They did NOT sign up for: general business news, unrelated productivity tips, or content outside of video creation and online expert business building.

## Awareness Level

**Solution-aware.** They know video content matters for building an audience and selling their expertise online. They may have tried creating videos and found it too time-consuming or the results too amateurish to reflect their professional level. They are aware that tools and systems exist to help, but may not yet be fully convinced that OneTake solves their specific problem.

## Primary Pain

They are losing time, customers, or confidence to video production. Either they are not publishing videos because editing takes too long, or the videos they publish feel amateur and do not reflect their expertise. They have a strong message to share but no efficient way to package it professionally and consistently.

## 3AM Question

"How am I supposed to publish consistent, professional video content every week — without spending days editing, hiring a team I can't afford, or sounding like every other generic content creator out there?"
